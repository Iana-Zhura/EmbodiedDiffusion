EmbodiedDiffusion
End-to-End Traversability-Guided Visual Diffusion
for Heterogeneous Robot Navigation

A single diffusion model that predicts traversability and generates feasible trajectories from one RGB image — prompt-free, real-time, and transferable across quadrupeds and aerial robots.

Method

Two components, one network

Distill VLM semantics into a lightweight student once. At deployment, the student runs prompt-free, while a FiLM-conditioned diffusion UNet produces the trajectory in 20 denoising steps.

①

VLM Teacher → Compact Student

AnyTraverse supplies embodiment-specific traversability maps as training targets. The student distills them once, offline — then runs prompt-free at 90 ms.

②

FiLM Embodiment Conditioning

The robot state vector modulates visual features channel-wise, so the same RGB scene yields platform-consistent traversability without per-platform models.

③

Planner-Free Synthetic Supervision

Smoothed Bezier paths between VLM-verified start–goal pairs supervise the diffusion UNet. The model learns the distribution of feasible trajectories — not memorized demos.

④

Traversability Regularization

𝓛trav concentrates denoising probability mass in visually safe regions — improving obstacle clearance by 26% over no-trav ablations.

Why It Matters

One model, many robots, zero prompts

⚡

Prompt-free at deployment

The VLM teacher is distilled once. The student never needs per-scene, per-embodiment prompt tuning at runtime — a prohibitive cost for real fleets.

🦾

Embodied by design

The same scene produces routes that go around narrow gates (quadruped) or through them (drone) — purely from FiLM state conditioning.

⏱

Real-time & adaptive

90 ms inference enables continuous replanning. Adapting to a new platform takes ~10 min of visual data — not a full retrain.

🧠

Distributes, not memorizes

Planner-free synthetic supervision teaches the distribution of feasible paths — so the model generalizes to gates it has never seen.
